IV THROMBOLYSIS CHECKLIST

This checklist covers standard 0–4.5 h IV thrombolysis ONLY.
Extended-window or wake-up stroke protocols are outside this sheet and require stroke consultant decision.

BASIC ELIGIBILITY

1. Suspected acute stroke?

2. Deficit is DISABLING (regardless of NIHSS)?

(e.g. aphasia, hemianopia, severe neglect, cannot walk, cannot use dominant hand)

3. Last Known Well (LKW) ≤ 4.5 hours?

ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS

17. Age ≥ 18 years confirmed?

18. Previous spontaneous ICH excluded? (unless structural cause clearly cured)

19. Clinical suspicion of SAH excluded?

(no thunderclap headache, no meningism, no unexplained ↓ consciousness)?

20. Active major internal bleeding and recent major GI bleed (< 3 weeks) excluded?

21. Intracranial or intraspinal surgery within last 3 months excluded?

22. Major non-cranial major surgery or major trauma within last 14 days excluded?

23. Severe bleeding diathesis excluded?

(e.g. severe thrombocytopenia, coagulation factor deficiency)?

24. Known or strongly suspected infective endocarditis with mycotic aneurysm risk excluded?

25. Aortic dissection clinically excluded?

(no sudden severe chest and/or back pain with pulse/BP asymmetry or hypotension)?

26. Therapeutic LMWH (e.g. Clexane full dose) within last 24 h excluded?

27. Severe head trauma with intracranial injury within last 3 months excluded?

28. Intra-axial malignant brain tumour excluded?
